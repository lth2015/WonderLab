Primary request: Create an extraordinarily detailed, coherent panoramic Chinese handscroll illustration of an ordinary spring day along the Bian River in late Northern Song Bianjing, inspired by the meticulous fine ink linework, pale mineral washes, dispersed elevated viewpoints and human detail density of Zhang Zeduan's Along the River During the Qingming Festival. An original new scene, not a reproduction of a specific painting or a museum digital scan.
Composition: very wide horizontal panorama, approximately 3:1. The river and both its banks connect a timber arch bridge on the left-center with a paper merchant's small open-front shop on the right, with connected tea stalls and cargo landing. An elevated, oblique, dispersed viewpoint, parallel architectural directions and readable shallow depth, never a fantasy aerial city. Fill the whole composition with interlocking everyday human activities and material detail, while retaining clear paths. River occupies the lower third with 3-4 varied cargo and passenger boats, ripples and reflections. Midground is the principal readable layer, small building groups, rough stone embankments, gently bending willows. Background town recedes in delicate haze rather than giant empty mountains. No artificial vignette.
People: approximately 40-60 small, individually drawn people in plausible Northern Song commoner robes, cloth headwraps and work garments, each with distinct silhouette and intelligible gestures. Four sailors coordinate the mooring of a cargo boat, porters transfer bamboo baskets of tea, a woman pours tea for two seated customers, a paper seller and a young apprentice press down the corners of loose paper, pedestrians pause on the bridge, a porter gives way to a handcart. Include subtle face direction, cloth folds, complete believable arms and legs. People are integrated at architectural scale, not enlarged portrait characters or identical symbols.
Materials: fine irregular warm charcoal and tea-brown ink outlines, subtly varied line weight, slightly dry brush edges, translucent layered watercolors and mineral pigment, warm pale unbleached silk-paper ground with restrained fine fiber grain. Roofs have individually legible but nonmechanical tile courses, weathered timber posts and mortise joints, thin bamboo screens, hanging blank cloth shop signs, stacks of paper, brush pots, paperweights, earthenware teapots and bowls, baskets tied with fiber rope, timber decking and rigging. Crisp local detail with gentle wash variation; realistic illustrated material specificity.

Constraints: aesthetic of a fine hand-painted historical scroll, NOT vector polygons, NOT clipart, NOT flat faceless stick figures, NOT a children's cartoon, NOT 3D, NOT photoreal cinema, NOT ukiyo-e.
